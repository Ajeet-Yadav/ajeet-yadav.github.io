About

From analytics depth to product ownership

I am a Platform Product Manager with a strong analytics foundation. I started in product analytics, turning behavioral data into decisions for products serving hundreds of millions of users, and now own data and AI products end to end at DeepIntent, a healthcare Ad Tech DSP.

My work sits where data science, engineering and go-to-market meet: forecasting engines, pricing and packaging systems, inventory quality, and experimentation. I am comfortable shipping probabilistic systems, setting guardrails, quality thresholds, fallbacks and clear UX to build trust when outputs are not always 100 percent correct.

I hold an M.Eng from BITS Pilani and am completing the Advanced Management Program in Business Analytics at the Indian School of Business (ISB), Hyderabad.
